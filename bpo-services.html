Omnichannel

Modern customer operations across voice and digital

Voice AI, digital automation, and human support — combined so routine moves fast and complex gets the right person.

A customer gets in touch — AI understands the request

From there, the path depends on how routine or complex it is.

Routine

AI handles or assists

  1. AI understands and classifies the request
  2. Provides the answer or completes a structured step
  3. Updates the system and confirms
Complex

Routed to a human

  1. Route to a trained human agent
  2. Provide the agent with full context
  3. Resolve the issue and update the system

Not every interaction should be automated — sensitive or exceptional cases always move to human support.
